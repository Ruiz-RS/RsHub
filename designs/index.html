Five directions for RsHub

These are mockups: you can navigate, open search with Ctrl+K and open settings, but nothing touches your PC.

  1. 1ObservatoryStar chart: ink blue, warm starlight, the components form a constellation.
  2. 2AluminumLight industrial hardware: buttons that sink, needle dials, LEDs.
  3. 3HUDA restrained gaming cockpit: a PC scan, beveled panels, odometer numbers.
  4. 4Native Windows 11Like a Microsoft app on its best day: Mica, Fluent, elements that fly between views.
  5. 5OrbitalThe showpiece: the components orbit the laptop in 3D and the camera travels to each module.
